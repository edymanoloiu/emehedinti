In Drobeta Turnu-Severin, a cultural festival celebrating local traditions concluded yesterday after four days of events featuring music, dance, and gastronomy. Organized by the municipal cultural center, the festival attracted thousands of visitors and helped promote regional heritage, offering workshops and exhibitions dedicated to Oltenian crafts and folklore. Local authorities underlined the event's role in boosting community engagement and tourism.

##  Accidents

A minor road accident occurred on 24 March at the intersection of Calea Severinului and Strada 1 Decembrie 1918, involving two passenger cars. The incident caused traffic delays for about an hour but resulted in no serious injuries. Police reported that the probable cause was failure to respect the right of way, and both drivers were issued warnings on site.

##  Economy

Local industry representatives from Drobeta Turnu-Severin participated in a regional business forum aimed at strengthening economic cooperation in Mehedinți County. The event, held at the Chamber of Commerce premises, focused on opportunities in the renewable energy sector and support for small and medium enterprises. Discussions highlighted potential new investments and public-private partnerships planned for the coming years.

##  Education

The Technical College of Drobeta Turnu-Severin announced the inauguration of a new robotics laboratory designed to enhance technical education and provide students with hands-on experience in automation technologies. This initiative is part of a broader educational modernization project supported by European funds. The new facility is expected to boost interest in STEM fields among local youth.

##  Urban Development

Municipal authorities revealed updated plans for the rehabilitation of several streets in the city center scheduled for mid-2026. The works will include improvements to pedestrian zones, modern lighting systems, and upgraded underground utilities. Residents are advised about possible temporary traffic restrictions during the execution period starting in May.

##  Environment

Cleanup campaigns continued along the Danube riverbanks under the coordination of local environmental organizations and volunteers. The latest effort focused on removing debris collected during winter months, aiming to preserve biodiversity and improve public access to green spaces within Drobeta Turnu-Severin. Officials praised community involvement and called for ongoing commitment to sustainable urban environments.

##  Local Politics

The city council convened on 24 March to debate new regulations on public space usage, particularly regarding outdoor markets and street fairs. Proposed changes seek to better organize these events, ensuring safety, cleanliness, and minimizing noise impact on residents. The discussion reflected efforts to balance commercial activities while maintaining urban quality of life.
