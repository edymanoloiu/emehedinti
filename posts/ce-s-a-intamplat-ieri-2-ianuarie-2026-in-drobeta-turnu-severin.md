In Drobeta Turnu-Severin, the local police conducted a controlled operation on January 2, aimed at combating petty theft and vandalism in several neighborhoods. Authorities arrested three individuals suspected of involvement in recent break-ins, helping to reassure residents concerned about rising street crime. Police continue investigations to identify other possible accomplices in the area.  

##  Accidents

A traffic accident occurred on the DN6 road near Drobeta Turnu-Severin late Friday afternoon, when a car collided with a motorcycle at an intersection. The motorcyclist suffered minor injuries and was transported to the Emergency Hospital for treatment. Traffic was temporarily disrupted, but normal flow resumed within two hours after clearance by emergency services.

##  Economy

The city council announced on January 3 the start of a public consultation regarding the expansion of the local industrial park. This initiative aims to attract new investors and boost job creation in the region by offering improved infrastructure and tax incentives. Officials emphasize the importance of community feedback to align development with residents' needs.

##  Education

Local schools welcomed students back for the first semester of 2026 this week, with several institutions updating their curriculum to include more digital skills training. The Municipal School Inspectorate revealed plans to increase funding for technology upgrades and teacher workshops, intending to prepare students better for the evolving job market. Parents and educators expressed support for the modernization efforts.

##  Urban Development

Renovation works resumed this weekend on the central pedestrian zone, focusing on pavement repairs and lighting improvements. The Municipality of Drobeta Turnu-Severin plans to finalize these upgrades by the end of February to enhance both safety and aesthetics for residents and visitors. Temporary detours are in place, with clear signage to minimize disruption.

##  Environment

The environmental department launched a new tree-planting campaign targeting public parks and green spaces across the city. Starting in mid-January, volunteers will assist in planting over 1,000 native trees to improve air quality and urban biodiversity. This effort aligns with the city’s broader sustainability goals set for 2030.

##  Local Politics

Mayor Ionel Popescu held a press conference on January 3, outlining priorities for the new year, including infrastructure modernization, support for small businesses, and increased transparency in local governance. He also invited citizens to participate in upcoming town hall meetings aimed at fostering more inclusive decision-making processes.

##  Conclusion

Today in Drobeta Turnu-Severin featured a balanced mix of community safety initiatives, economic development plans, and ongoing urban improvements. The local administration's focus on modernization and public engagement was evident across multiple sectors, reflecting a proactive approach to enhancing residents' quality of life.
