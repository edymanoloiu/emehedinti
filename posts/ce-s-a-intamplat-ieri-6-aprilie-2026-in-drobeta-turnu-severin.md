Local authorities reported minor injuries and temporary traffic disruptions while the traffic police investigated the cause. Emergency medical teams responded promptly, and the injured were taken to the municipal hospital for treatment.

City planners emphasize that completion is expected by late 2027, promising enhanced urban appeal.
